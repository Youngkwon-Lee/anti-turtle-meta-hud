# Clinical and measurement limits

## What the prototype measures

In head-only mode, the prototype uses glasses orientation to estimate absolute head pitch deviation from a personal neutral baseline. In hybrid mode, it compares head pitch with a separate torso pitch reference.

## What it does not measure

The displayed value is not clinical craniovertebral angle (CVA). Standard photographic CVA requires a sagittal view and landmarks at the tragus and C7. Glasses orientation alone cannot locate those landmarks and may not distinguish neck translation from whole-body movement.

## Thresholds

The GOOD, HOLD/CAUTION, and CORRECT NOW/BAD thresholds are prototype coaching defaults. They are not diagnostic cutoffs and should not be generalized across ages, symptoms, body types, or clinical populations.

## Appropriate use

Use the project for interface research, sensor experiments, wellness coaching prototypes, and controlled demonstrations. Do not use it to diagnose, treat, triage, or monitor a medical condition.

Any clinical study should define its protocol in advance and report calibration, failure rate, latency, repeatability, measurement error, subgroup error, and comparison against an appropriate reference method.
