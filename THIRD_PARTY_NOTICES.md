MediaPipe is distributed under the Apache License 2.0; see the [MediaPipe repository](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe) and its [license](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/LICENSE).

Pose inference runs in the browser and Anti Turtle does not transmit or retain camera frames. The runtime and model are downloaded from jsDelivr and Google Storage, and the MediaPipe project documents performance and utilization metrics that may be sent to Google. Users should review the upstream [MediaPipe Tasks Vision privacy notice](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/mediapipe/tasks/web/vision/README.md) before enabling the preview.
